# One Word From Your Ex

My entry to the make.fun competition "One Word From Your Ex": a single
word — the one my ex would reach for if she had to sum me up in a single
breath.

The word lives in `line.txt`. Running `main.py` copies it, untouched, to
`/output/entry.txt`, which is all the contest asks for: one word, nothing
else. She never called me her destination. I was where she waited until
her real flight boarded.
